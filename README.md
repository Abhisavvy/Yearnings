# Yearnings: playtest build

Play it here: https://abhisavvy.github.io/Yearnings/

This is an early playtest build of a puzzle game. Open it on a phone and hold the phone upright. For a full-screen view, use Share and then Add to Home Screen.

## How to play

1. Drag a paint tin from a queue column onto the window to paint.
2. A tin that cannot finish hangs on a hook. If the hooks are full, the level fails. You then get a few choices, and you can retry.
3. Each level turns the window into its next moment. At the end of a chapter you see a strip of pictures. You can rate the chapter from 1 to 5 stars and leave a comment. Both are optional.
4. Settings is the sliders icon at the top right. It has Vibration (Android phones; iPhone is untested), Sound and colour patterns. Home has your latest painting and the Shelf with the pictures you have painted.
5. The game remembers where you are. Settings > Reset progress clears your play data and progress and keeps your settings.

## Feedback and data

- Everything stays on your phone. The game makes no network calls and has no accounts.
- It keeps an anonymous play log for each level: the result, time played, moves, hooks used, powerups used and the keepsake colour chosen. It also records screen size, whether the device has touch, the Vibration and Sound settings and your chapter ratings.
- Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to send it. To send it, go to Settings > Send feedback > Copy, then paste the text into a message to whoever shared this link with you.
